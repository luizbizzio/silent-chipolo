# Security Policy

This repository is an **unofficial hardware modification guide** for the Chipolo LOOP. It does not provide firmware, software, or a tracking service.

## Supported Content

Only the latest version of this guide is maintained. Older copies may have outdated steps or safety information.

## Safety and Privacy

Removing the sound disc disables **all audible alerts**, including the ring feature and audible unwanted-tracking safety alerts.

- Only modify a device you own or have permission to modify.
- **Never use a modified tracker to follow or locate someone without their knowledge and consent.**
- Do not assume the tracker still has its original water resistance after opening it.
- Be careful around the lithium battery. Do not puncture, bend, or damage it.
- This guide does not guarantee that a modified device will work as before.

## Report a Security or Safety Concern

If you find a privacy risk, a safety problem, or incorrect information in this guide:

1. Use GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** option under the repository's **Security** tab, if available.
2. If private reporting is not available, open a general issue asking for a private way to share the details. **Do not post sensitive information or instructions for misuse publicly.**

Please include the affected section, a short description of the problem, its possible impact, and a suggested correction if you have one.

Do not share personal tracking data, passwords, account details, or other private information.

## Scope

This repository only documents a physical modification. For security issues involving Chipolo's official firmware, apps, accounts, or services, contact **Chipolo** through its official support channels.

This project is not affiliated with or approved by Chipolo.
